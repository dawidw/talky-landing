Bring your own key

Your own key in 2 minutes

Talky transcribes on your Mac and builds meeting summaries on your Mac with Apple's on-device model. If you want frontier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, or Groq, add your own API key for dictation clean-up and Summary.

1

Create an account

Sign up with a provider such as OpenAI or Groq. It takes about a minute.

2

Create and copy a key

Open the API keys page, press Create new key, and copy it.

3

Paste it into Talky

Open Preferences, find Text improvement, paste the key and press Test key.

4

You are done

Dictation clean-up and Summary can use your provider. Your key stays in the macOS Keychain on your Mac.

Optional. Without a key, dictation and Summary run fully on your Mac. You can remove the key any time in Preferences.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is an API key?

A private password that lets an app use an AI model from a provider such as OpenAI or Groq. You create it in your provider account and paste it into Talky.

Do I need it at all?

No. Talky dictates fully on your Mac without a key. A key only adds fuller punctuation and grammar clean-up.

Does my voice leave my Mac?

No. Speech is transcribed on your Mac. If you add a key, only the transcribed text is sent to your provider for clean-up, never audio.

Is it safe?

Your key is stored in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. You can remove it in Preferences, or revoke it in your provider account at any time.

How much does it cost?

Talky is a one-time purchase of $XX. With a key, you pay your provider only for what you use. For typical dictation that is usually under $1 a month.

Which provider should I pick?

Use the one you already have an account with. If you are starting fresh, OpenAI or Groq both work and take a couple of minutes to set up.

Are meetings included?

Yes. Meeting notes with speaker labels are part of the one-time price, with no extra plan.
