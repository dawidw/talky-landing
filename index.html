How it works

From thought to text in three moves

No dock icon required. The pill shows state while you work in any app.

1

Hold ⌃⇧

Press and hold your dictation shortcut. The pill expands and listens.

2

Speak

Talk naturally. Voice memos and meetings use their own shortcuts when you need them.

3

Let go

Release the keys. Talky pastes at your cursor and saves to the scratchpad.

Features

Dictation, memos, and meetings in one Mac app

Dictation anywhere

Hold to talk in Slack, Mail, Notes, or any text field. Transcription runs locally with Parakeet on the Neural Engine.

Voice memos

Record a thought without aiming at a text field. Saved to scratchpad with search and copy.

Meetings without a bot

Capture your mic and system audio from Zoom or Meet. No participant joins the call. Speaker labels on the transcript. Summaries with Action items and Decisions, plus your own summary instructions in Settings.

Scratchpad and history

Every dictation, memo, and meeting in one searchable timeline. Summaries stay next to the transcript.

On your Mac

Audio is transcribed locally. No Talky account. Optional API key only if you want cloud summary or punctuation polish on text.

Your summary rules

On-device summaries on Apple Intelligence (macOS 26), or your provider with a key you add. Custom instructions for tone and structure.

Transcription models

Small default, bigger options in Settings

Every model runs on your Mac. Recordings are not uploaded for transcription.

Default

NVIDIA Parakeet TDT v3

Talky ships with Parakeet TDT v3 because it is compact and quick on Apple silicon. It covers Polish and the other European languages in the language picker.

Whisper large-v3 turbo

Download optional in Settings. Runs through WhisperKit when you want a larger Whisper model without the full size of large-v3.

Whisper large-v3

Also optional in Settings. The most accurate Whisper option in Talky. Slower and heavier on disk and memory than turbo or Parakeet.

Switch models in Talky Settings. Dictation still works offline after the model you chose is on your Mac.

Privacy

Your voice stays on your Mac

Talky does not run cloud transcription and does not require an account. Summaries can stay on-device when Apple Intelligence is available.

Stays local

  • Microphone and meeting audio until transcription finishes
  • Transcripts and scratchpad notes in local storage
  • Preferences and optional API key in the Keychain

What Talky sends

  • Update check (optional): the app may fetch a public appcast for new versions via Sparkle. That request can include your IP address and user agent. No notes or audio.
  • Your API provider (optional): only transcribed text you send for polish or summary, never audio, if you add a key.
  • Otherwise: no analytics, no crash SDK, no sign-in to Talky.

Bring your own key

Optional cloud polish on your terms

Talky transcribes on your Mac. Add your own API key only if you want stronger punctuation polish or summaries from OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, and others.

1

Create an account

Sign up with a provider such as OpenAI or Groq.

2

Create and copy a key

Open the API keys page, create a key, and copy it.

3

Paste it into Talky

Open Preferences, paste the key, and run Test key.

4

You are done

The key stays in the macOS Keychain. Remove it any time. Audio never goes to the provider.

Without a key, dictation stays fully local. Summaries can use on-device Apple Intelligence on macOS 26.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When is Talky available?

Coming soon. This page will update when the Mac app is ready.

Which macOS versions are supported?

macOS 14.2 or later. System audio capture for meetings uses the System Audio Recording permission from macOS 14.2. On-device meeting summaries need Apple Intelligence on macOS 26.

Which languages are supported?

25 European languages plus Auto detection, with a default and fallback language in Settings. Summaries can follow the transcript or your summary language preference.

Does Talky join my meeting as a bot?

No. Talky captures microphone and system audio on your Mac. Nothing joins the call as a participant.

Does my voice leave my Mac?

No for transcription. Audio is processed locally. If you add an API key, only text you send for polish or summary goes to that provider.

Do I need an API key?

No. Dictation works without one. A key is optional for text polish and for summaries when you do not use on-device Apple Intelligence.

What is an API key?

A secret from a provider such as OpenAI or Groq. You create it in their dashboard and paste it into Talky Preferences.

Does Talky work offline?

Yes for dictation after the local model is installed. Summaries need on-device Apple Intelligence or a network call to your provider if you use a key.
