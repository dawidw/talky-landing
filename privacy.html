Short version. Talky runs on your Mac. Audio and transcripts stay on your Mac. The app does not collect analytics, does not use accounts, and does not send telemetry. If you add your own API key, only transcribed text you choose to improve is sent to that provider.

Who we are

Talky is made and sold by plain d. Dawid Woźniak, a sole proprietorship (JDG) registered in Poland, which is the controller for anything covered by this policy. Contact: dawid@plaind.pl.

What the app does

Talky is a native macOS app for hold-to-talk dictation, a scratchpad, and meeting transcription. Speech is transcribed locally on your Mac using the Parakeet model. The app may access the microphone, accessibility for pasting text, and screen or system audio only when you start meeting capture.

What stays on your Mac

  • Audio recorded for dictation or meetings, until Talky finishes processing it
  • Transcripts and scratchpad notes in local storage on your Mac
  • Preferences such as hotkeys, language, and whether to start at login
  • An optional API key, stored in the macOS Keychain if you add one

None of this is uploaded to Talky servers, because Talky does not operate cloud accounts for the app.

Optional API key (bring your own key)

If you turn on text improvement and add a key from a provider such as OpenAI or Groq, Talky sends only the transcribed text to that provider to add punctuation and grammar clean-up. Audio is never sent. That processing is governed by the provider’s privacy policy and terms, not this one. You can remove the key in Preferences or revoke it with the provider at any time.

What we do not collect

Talky has no in-app analytics, no crash reporting SDK, no advertising identifiers, and no sign-in. We do not receive your voice, transcripts, or API traffic.

Update checks

The app may download a public appcast file, using Sparkle, to see whether a newer version exists. That request carries only what any download does: your IP address and a user agent. It does not include your notes, audio, or API key. Whoever hosts the file may log the request under their own policy. We do not receive or use those logs.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage, and loads no analytics or third party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., which may log visitors’ IP addresses under its own policy. We do not receive or use those logs.

Purchases

When you buy Talky, payment is handled by our merchant of record (Creem). They process your email, payment details, and license delivery under their privacy policy. We receive only what is needed to confirm the sale and support you, such as your email and order reference.

Your rights

The app itself does not profile you. If you email us, we use that message only to reply. You can ask us to access, correct or delete it by writing to dawid@plaind.pl. You can also complain to your data protection authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
